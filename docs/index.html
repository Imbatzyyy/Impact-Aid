Impact Aid helps people in the Philippines prepare for disasters with practical field guides, practice quizzes and a directory of local emergency facilities.

Download for Android

Preparation in your pocket

  • Read six illustrated guides and watch their lesson videos.
  • Practice earthquake, tsunami, typhoon, flood, landslide and volcanic eruption questions. Track your own progress.
  • Find hospitals, police, fire stations, rescue teams, local government offices and mapped evacuation facilities.

Your account

Use email or Google to access your private learning record and saved contacts. Google sign-in requests only your basic profile and email to identify you. Supabase stores your account and learning data.

Public emergency numbers and the facility directory are available without signing in. The app opens your phone dialer; you place the call. It does not dispatch responders or show live emergency conditions.
