Terms of use

Effective November 14, 2026

Impact Aid is an educational preparedness app and a directory. Use it to learn, plan and find contact information.

During an emergency

Follow current instructions from authorities. The app does not dispatch responders, monitor incidents, guarantee routes are safe or confirm that a shelter is active. Listings and telephone numbers may be incomplete or outdated. Confirm services and safe access with the facility or local government when possible.

Learning and accounts

Keep sign-in details and email codes private. Use the app responsibly and do not interfere with other accounts or services. Course quizzes and certificates record learning in this app; they are not professional emergency-response qualifications.

Connectivity and availability

Account functions, saving progress and loading map tiles require internet access. Calls require phone service. The phone determines location accuracy, which may be reduced indoors or with approximate-location permission. Keep an emergency plan that can be used without the app.

Data and content

Read the privacy information for account and Google data use. Map data is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors under the ODbL license. Source and attribution information appears in the application.

Questions or account requests: Impact Aid support.
