Privacy information

Effective November 14, 2026

This page describes how Impact Aid uses information to provide its Android preparedness app.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google, Google sends Supabase an account identifier, verified email and basic profile details such as your name and profile image URL. We use this information to authenticate you, create your Impact Aid profile or associate the sign-in with an existing account. We do not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, calendars or Google Contacts. The app does not use your Google data for advertising.

Account and learning information

Supabase stores your email, authentication information, full name, username, optional age, mobile number, address, your chosen profile photo, lesson completions, reading position, semester quiz assignments, answers, scores and personal contacts that you save. Database access policies restrict each account to its own private records. A new Google profile uses your Google name; you can edit your profile details in the app. The app does not use your Google picture as your chosen profile photo. Photos you choose are resized and re-encoded before uploading to a private Supabase storage bucket; the original file and its EXIF metadata are not uploaded. Authenticated storage rules restrict photo downloads to the owning account.

Philippine address suggestions

PSGC Cloud supplies public city, municipality and barangay names. Your search text is matched on your device. The service receives the selected city code when the app retrieves its barangays, together with normal connection information. It does not receive your name, house, street, age, mobile number or saved profile. A bundled city reference and cached barangays help if the service is unavailable. You can always type an address manually.

Your location

Only when you request location, the app uses your phone’s estimated position to center the map and calculate nearby distances. It does not upload GPS coordinates to your Supabase profile. OpenStreetMap receives map tile requests. Directions open Google Maps with a facility’s coordinates. These providers receive normal connection information, such as IP address.

Service providers and sharing

Google handles Google sign-in, Supabase handles authentication and account data, and Resend delivers verification and recovery emails. Account information is shared with these providers as needed for their functions. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK. The project does not sell personal data.

On your device

The app stores its sign-in session, unfinished quiz answers and a random installation identifier used for semester question sets. The identifier is not a hardware identifier. Your certificate contains your display name; you decide where to save or share it. Device storage should be protected using the phone’s security controls.

Retention and your choices

Your learning records stay associated with your account until deleted. You can edit or clear optional profile details, replace or remove your chosen photo, delete your saved contacts, and sign out. For account information, an export, correction or deletion, contact Impact Aid support using the email below. We may need to verify your account ownership before acting. Administrative backup copies, where present, may remain until they are replaced or expire.

You can revoke Google access through your Google Account’s third-party connections page. Revoking access does not itself delete records already stored in Impact Aid; contact support for account deletion. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings and still browse the directory.

Questions or account requests: Impact Aid support.
